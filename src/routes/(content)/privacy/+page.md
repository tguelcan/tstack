## Who is responsible

The controller for the processing described here is the operator of this
service. You reach us at the address in the [imprint](/imprint).

## What we store

- **Account data** — the name and email address you enter when you register,
  plus a hash of your password. We never store the password itself.
- **Workspace content** — everything you and your team put into the product.
- **Technical data** — IP address, browser and timestamp of each request, kept
  in server logs for a limited period so we can investigate abuse and errors.

## Why we are allowed to

We process account and workspace data to provide the service you signed up for.
Log data is kept on the basis of our legitimate interest in operating the
service securely.

## How long we keep it

Account and workspace data live as long as your account does. After you delete
it, backups take up to 30 days to age out. Server logs are deleted after 14
days.

## Who else sees it

Only the processors we need to run the service — hosting, email delivery and
payments. Each of them is bound by a data processing agreement and none of them
may use your data for their own purposes.

## What you can ask for

You can request a copy of your data, have it corrected or deleted, object to
processing, and complain to a supervisory authority. Write to us and we will
answer within a month.

## Cookies

We set one cookie, and it holds your session. There is no tracking and no
analytics in this template. Your theme and sidebar preferences live in
`localStorage` and never leave your browser.
